Presentation
Investigation Scope

Resource & Location

  • Location: Hope Street and nearby residential areas, Taytayan, San Carlos City.
  • Resource Investigated: Water resource (river / sapa).
  • Community Importance: Serves as a natural drainage channel carrying rainwater away, reducing flood risks, protecting homes, and directing water safely to the coast.
Current Condition

System Status & Issues

The sapa is still functioning well, but regular cleaning and proper waste disposal are needed to prevent pollution and blockages.

Core Issue: Recurrent seasonal overflow of the creek canal system directly into surrounding residential zones due to narrow channel widths unable to handle sudden high-velocity stormwater volumes.
Hydrological Analysis

Causes & Effects of Overflow

Understanding rainfall volume, water velocity, and community hazards.

Heavy Rainfall

Strong rainfall brings massive water inflows into creeks. If capacity is exceeded, flooding affects nearby houses.

PAGASA Data: The Philippines receives 2,000–3,000 mm of rainfall annually, peaking during rainy seasons.

High Water Velocity

Fast-moving water carries larger flow volume quickly, rapidly raising creek water levels beyond natural capacity.

Hydrology Fact: Unchecked high-velocity runoff overflows near bridges, endangering residents and the local environment.

Side Effects & Hazards

  • • Damage to houses and properties.
  • • Contaminated floodwater.
  • • Possible spread of waterborne diseases.
  • • Mandatory evacuation risks.

Reference: World Health Organization (WHO) flood health risks.

Community Engagement

Target Audience

Residents of Hope St., Taytayan, San Carlos City, high school students, and households near the sapa.

  • Why Chosen: Students live near the water daily and can spread awareness to families; daily waste habits directly impact the sapa.
  • Expected Role: Join clean-ups, educate peers, and reduce plastic waste.
Proposed Projects

Preventive Action Plan

  • Clean-up Drives: Regularly clear trash and blockages from the sapa.
  • Proper Waste Disposal: Use garbage bins instead of dumping in waterways.
  • Tree Planting: Plant trees along stream banks to prevent soil erosion.
